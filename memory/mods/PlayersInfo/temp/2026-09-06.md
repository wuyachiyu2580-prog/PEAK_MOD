- `AfflictionIconDisplayMode` has three choices: `ShowAll` (default), `HideTeammates`, and `HideAll`. English/Chinese BepInEx and ModConfig labels, descriptions, and enum names are synchronized; Chinese enum names are `不隐藏图标`, `隐藏队友图标`, and `隐藏全部图标`.
- Teammate icon references are limited to the cloned teammate tree. Local hiding is limited to local vanilla HUD affliction icons and does not touch extra stamina, shield, campfire, or inventory icons.
- Distance filtering resolves finite safe positions: normal/`passedOut`/`fullyPassedOut` use the current torso; `dead` uses the last living `VirtualCenter`. A single invalid character position is skipped. Missing-roster retention does not apply to a roster member that is simply out of range; 5 m hysteresis remains.

- Low-frequency updates are unified at `0.25s`; responsive bar animation remains separately handled.
- Diagnostic snapshots, bindings, build details, clone validation, and layout probes are behind `Advanced.DebugLogging`. The reviewed log had no PlayersInfo warning/error entries; old `[PI-DIAG]` lines predated the gating change.
